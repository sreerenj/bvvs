Our story

A heritage Ayurvedic institution rooted in Kerala's healing traditions, blending classical wisdom with modern care.

Legacy of Healing

From a village clinic to a respected Ayurvedic brand.

Vaidyakalanidhi Sri. N. Bhaskaran Nair, a visionary in Ayurveda with unwavering commitment and profound healing potential, started a small village clinic in 1941. Bhaskara Vilasam Vaidyasala has been a trusted name in Ayurvedic medicine for over eight decades.

Its origins trace back to the rich cultural and medicinal heritage of Nedumangadu, where traditional healing practices have been passed down through generations. The institution has grown from a local vaidya's clinic into a respected brand known for purity, efficacy and ethical practice.

Ayurvedic expertise

The Vaidyasala manufactures Ayurvedic and Unani preparations: herbal remedies, oils, tonics and therapeutic products, made from time-tested formulations in authentic texts. We now produce more than 250 classical preparations without losing their authenticity, and we develop new formulations referenced from authoritative Ayurvedic scriptures.

Milestones

Eight decades of practice.

  • 1941

    Founded as a small village clinic in Nedumangadu by Vaidyakalanidhi N. Bhaskaran Nair.

  • Growth

    Expanded into manufacturing of classical Ayurvedic and Unani preparations, with a hospital, branch and agencies.

  • 2023

    Incorporated as Bhaskara Vilasam Vaidyasala Private Limited, with a registered office in Thiruvananthapuram.
