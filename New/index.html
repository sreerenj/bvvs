Our Heritage

A village clinic that became a trusted Ayurvedic name.

Vaidyakalanidhi N. Bhaskaran Nair started Bhaskara Vilasam as a small village clinic in Nedumangadu. Today it is a private limited company that still follows the classical texts, with the same commitment to purity, efficacy and ethical practice.
