What we make

Time-tested formulations, authentic to the texts.

Bhaskara Vilasam manufactures Ayurvedic and Unani pharmaceutical preparations: herbal remedies, oils, tonics and therapeutic products. Each is crafted from formulations in authentic texts and refined through years of clinical experience.

  • Medicated oils (Keram / Thailam)
  • Lehyams and tonics, such as Chyavanaprasam
  • Choornams and Vadakams
  • Kuzhambus and other classical forms
  • New formulations referenced from authoritative scriptures
